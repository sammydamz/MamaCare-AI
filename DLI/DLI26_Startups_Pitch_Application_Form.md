# DLI26 Startups Pitch — Application Form

**Deep Learning Indaba 2026 Startup Pitch Competition**

## Overview
The Startup Pitch Competition showcases innovative African startups using AI, Data Science, and related technologies to solve real-world problems. Selected startups pitch to a panel of judges, investors, VCs, and industry stakeholders, with prizes including entry into other international pitch competitions and cloud credits.

**Note:** Open only to applicants already accepted to attend the DLI.

## Key Dates
| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Application Deadline | 13 July 2026 |
| Selection Notification | Before 24 July 2026 |

## Evaluation Criteria
- Startup maturity and traction
- Innovation and impact
- Feasibility and scalability
- Team composition, diversity, and expertise

---

## Application Form Fields

### Applicant Info
- **Full Name** *(required)*
- **Email Address** *(required)*
- **Registered for DLI via Baobab platform?** — Yes / No *(required)*
  - If yes → email used for registration
- **Country of Residence** *(required)*

### Startup Info
- **Startup Name** *(required)*
- **Startup Website** (if applicable) *(required)*
- **Startup Headquarters Country** *(required)*
- **Number of Founding Team Members** *(required)*
- **Team Members** (Name, Role, Country) *(required)*
- **Do all attending team members have DLI registration?** — Yes / No / Not Yet *(required)*
- **Sector / Industry** *(required)*
- **Startup Stage** *(required)* — Idea / Prototype / MVP / Early Revenue / Growth Stage / Scaling

### Pitch Content
- **Innovation Description** (max 50 words) *(required)*
- **What problem does your startup address?** *(required)*
- **What makes your solution unique?** *(required)*
- **What impact has your startup achieved or expects to achieve?** *(required)*

### Funding & Recognition
- **Received any funding?** — Yes / No *(required)*
  - If yes → funding source(s) and amount(s)
- **Received awards, recognitions, grants, fellowships, or accelerator support?** — Yes / No *(required)*
  - If yes → details

### Pitch Deck Upload
- **Format:** PDF only, max 10 MB *(required)*
- **Length:** Max 10 slides (excluding cover/appendix)
- **Must address:**
  1. Problem Statement
  2. Solution & Innovation
  3. Product Demonstration or MVP
  4. Market Opportunity
  5. Business Model
  6. Traction & Key Metrics
  7. Impact
  8. Team
  9. Funding History (if any)
  10. Future Vision & Growth Strategy

### Consents & Declarations
- **Consent to share pitch deck with investors/VCs/stakeholders** — I Agree / I Do Not Agree *(required)*
- **Available to pitch in person during DLI?** — Yes / No *(required)*
- **Declaration of accuracy** — I Agree *(required)*
